The Story of Kiyafet

A journey born between two friends — fueled by passion, creativity, and a dream that began on Instagram.

How Kiyafet Began

Kiyafet was founded by two friends who shared a deep passion for fashion, design, and the excitement of building something meaningful together. What began as casual conversations between them soon turned into a vision — a vision to create a brand that blends modern Indian elegance with thoughtful craftsmanship.

With limited resources but limitless passion, they began their journey in the most authentic way — by launching an Instagram page in early 2025. From the very first post, they aimed to create styles that feel expressive, comfortable, and personal.

What started with two friends and a shared dream has now grown into a brand supported by a community that believes in simplicity, elegance, and individuality. Kiyafet is more than fashion — it is a story stitched with passion and friendship.
